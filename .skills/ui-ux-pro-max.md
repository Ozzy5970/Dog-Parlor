# UI/UX Pro Max Guidance

This guide defines the standards for building high-quality, polished, branded interfaces for Groomers Dog Parlour, ensuring all design elements avoid generic AI-created characteristics and feel premium, cohesive, and intentional.

## 1. Core Principles

*   **Avoid AI-Slop / Generic Layouts**: Do not generate generic feature grids, abstract hero headers, or generic mockups. Every layout section must feel bespoke, hand-crafted, and highly relevant to dog grooming.
*   **Strong Visual Hierarchy**: Establish clear visual order. The main heading (H1) must instantly communicate value, subheadings must group information logically, and call-to-action (CTA) buttons must stand out clearly.
*   **Brand-Led Identity**: 
    *   Utilize a rich, tailored palette inspired by the **Groomers Crimson / Red** brand identity.
    *   Complement the primary red with warm neutrals (amber, peach, soft slate) instead of cold, clinical greys.
    *   Avoid generic plain colors; use harmonious HSL/RGB combinations (e.g., crimson primary, rose highlights, warm white card backgrounds).
*   **Tasteful Motion**: Use micro-animations and smooth transitions to make the page feel responsive and alive, not distracting. Avoid chaotic motion or loops.
*   **User-Centric Spacing & Grid**: Maintain generous padding (e.g., `py-16` or `py-20` on landing page sections) to give content room to breathe. Use consistent grid intervals (Tailwind gaps, flex spacing).
*   **Conversion and Clarity**: Every section must serve a purpose. Ensure layout items focus on:
    *   *Trust*: Testimonials, local badges, and hygiene standards.
    *   *Clarity*: Pricing tables, simple description lists, and service limits.
    *   *Action*: Prominent, descriptive CTAs (e.g., "Book Grooming Now").

---

## 2. Layout & Typography Rules

*   **Card Styling**: Use cards with soft borders (`border-slate-200/80` or `border-rose-100/50`) and subtle shadows (`shadow-xs` or `shadow-sm`) to compartmentalize information.
*   **Typography Pairing**: Pairing modern fonts from Google Fonts:
    *   Headings: A clean, friendly sans-serif with personality (e.g., *Plus Jakarta Sans*, *Outfit*, or *Inter*).
    *   Body: High-legibility text font (e.g., *Inter* or *Plus Jakarta Sans*) with bold font weights (`font-semibold` or `font-bold`) for labels.
*   **Gradients**: Avoid heavy multi-stop gradients across text or full screens. Use subtle radial background glows or soft, single-directional fades.
*   **Empty States**: Avoid leaving screens completely blank. Use custom illustrations (e.g., Lucide icon configurations) with friendly, helpful descriptive copy.
